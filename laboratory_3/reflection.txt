The easiest design to me was the square post. I made the "Hello!" big for contrast and to make use of white space. I applied the color scheme and the fonts from the branding kit. I ensured readability by making important texts bigger and not cluttering the image with too much information. Making sure that the reading order is based on how important it is. I learned that creating social media graphics can be difficult if you don't have ideas or aren't able to apply the basic concepts of graphic design.
